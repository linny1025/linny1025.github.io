TL Hanna Defeats BHP

BHP took on the TL Hanna Yellow Jackets for the season opener. BHP held a strong lead of 14-0 through the first half but was unable to maintain momemtum through the second half. With a sad lost of 14-17, BHP ultimately lost by a field goal by Corban Lack with seconds left in the game.

Although a devestating lose for the Bears, their freshman quarterback, Rylan Crooks, shows a promising season for the Bears. Throwing two touchdowns including an 80 yard pass, we are excited to see what the future holds for this young athlete.

BHP Head Coach, David Crane, States "This is our year."

We met with BHP's new head coach, David Crane, after the game. He stated,We have some work to do, but this is our year. We have a solid offense, we just need to do some defense work. TL Hanna played a great game, but we will be ready for them next time.

BHP will face the JL Mann High School on Friday, September 4 at BHP.
